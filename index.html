Thrifted with intention, not just volume

We started PairPerfection because most secondhand shopping online is a scroll of mystery bundles and blurry photos. We wanted the opposite: a small, tightly-edited feed where every listing has real photos, an honest condition note, and a price that makes sense.

Every item is handled, checked and photographed by us before it goes up — nothing is listed sight unseen. New drops go up on the feed first, and once a piece sells, it's gone for good.

Hand-picked

We go through every rack ourselves — no drop-shipped bundles.

Condition checked

Stains, tears and wear are noted upfront, not left for you to find.

One of one

Everything is a single piece. If it's sold, it won't come back.

How buying works

No website checkout — everything happens over Instagram.

  1. 1

    Browse the feed & highlights

    New pieces go up as posts, with price and size in the caption. Highlights hold anything still in stock.

  2. 2

    DM the piece you want

    Send us the post — first DM holds it for 24 hours while we confirm details with you.

  3. 3

    We confirm price & delivery

    Shipping cost or local pickup / pop-up meetup, whichever works for you.

  4. 4

    Pay & it's yours

    Once payment's confirmed, we pack it up and it's on its way to you.

Good for your wardrobe, good for the planet

Buying secondhand keeps a wearable piece of clothing out of a landfill and out of the demand for another new one to be made. It's a small swap, but it adds up — one worn-in jacket at a time.

Less waste

Clothes that still have plenty of life left get worn again instead of thrown out.

Better value

Well-made pieces, often from brands you'd pay far more for new.

Actually unique

No two people ever end up in the exact same outfit off our feed.
